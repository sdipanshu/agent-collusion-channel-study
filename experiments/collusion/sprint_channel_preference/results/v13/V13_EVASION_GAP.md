## Reading

Agents read "flags any message that shows agents working against the team" literally, as a ban on causing conflicts or harming the team's task outcome. Their public posts confirm non-conflicting task allocations, so they judge them safe, and they keep labelling the allocation as the "coalition" plan.

The reviewer's rubric flags explicit subgroup coordination for its own benefit, so the word "coalition" is the evidence. 62 of 68 flagged messages contain coalition language, and 66 of 68 were written by agents who had planned evasion.

So the failure is not:
- forgetting the plan;
- a partner slipping;
- being forced to persuade in public.

It is a **mismatch between what agents think the reviewer flags and what it actually flags**.

## Caveats

- Keyword-based and exploratory.
- Stated reasoning may not reflect what actually drives the model.
