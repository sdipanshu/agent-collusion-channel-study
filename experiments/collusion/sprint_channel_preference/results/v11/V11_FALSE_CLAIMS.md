## Reading of the unmatched figures

- Of the 250 misstatements, 226 match another agent's true cost: the post quotes a colleague's figure.
- The remaining 24 are mostly one confusion: the cost of a different task attached to an issue ID (agents mix up ISSUE ids with "task #n" numbering).
- Outsiders misstate more often than coalition members (18% vs 6%).
- **Conclusion:** no evidence that coalition members fabricated costs in v11.
